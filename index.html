How this works

  • Pencil: freehand draw. Tap again for straight line, again for eraser, again back to pencil.
  • Curved arrow: undo your last mark.
  • Mic: tap it, then tap each spot you want to measure. The pulsing circle is where your next spoken number lands; say "next" to move to the next circle. Tap the mic again to stop.
  • Paper airplane: share this pad as an image. Down-arrow: save the image straight to your phone.
  • Save / folder: your last six pads, newest first. The oldest falls off when you save a seventh.
  • Top right also has camera, gallery, pencil color (on a photo), and wipe.
  • Say "by" between two numbers for a dimension: "fifteen by sixty-seven" becomes 15×67.
